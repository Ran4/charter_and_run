## CONTRACT CHANGES
1. **The apron and runway ground sit at `PLANE_GROUND_Y = −4.65` (plane-local), not y 0.13.**
   - Why: at 0.13 the wings (y −0.85) and the belly would be buried in the snow.
   - Now the plane stands on its gear, A1's lounge reads as the upper departures floor of a pier (my lower storey fills the space underneath), and the Gando strip "rises" to −4.65 at touchdown.
   - Visual only; no collision is involved.
   - A1, if you add any gate exterior at ground level, put it at −4.65. Anything outside the lounge walls must stay ≤ y 2.6.
   - The port wing tip passes 2 cm under the lounge slab's SW corner, at x 13–15, z −1…1. Keep that corner free below y −0.25.
2. **Pitch sign: flightScenery owns `scenery.rotation.x`.** I reasoned it out rather than tested it; there is no screenshot of the cabin's own value.
   - In three.js a positive rotation.x lowers what is ahead (nose up). So cabin.js's takeoff `−0.12` and approach `+0.04` would show nose-down on takeoff and nose-up on approach.
   - `tick()` rewrites rotation.x every frame from the phase clock: takeoff +0.12 from t 8, approach −0.03, the landing flare, and so on. The cabin's writes have no visible effect as long as `scenery.tick` runs after them (it does in Phase 0 cabin.js).
   - Proposed patch for cabin.js (A1/Phase 2): delete the two `scenery.scenery.rotation.x = …` lines in `setPhase` and `stepPhase`.
   - Proposed patch for DESIGN §5.6: change "pitch → −0.12 rad" to "pitch +0.12 rad (scenery.rotation.x, positive = nose up), owned by flightScenery".
3. **`buildPlaneExterior(group, { gear = true, cutaway = false })`.** The `cutaway` option is additive; the default is the closed plane D2 needs.
   - New export `PLANE_GROUND_Y` (−4.65).
   - For D2: `grp.position.set(240, 0.15 - PLANE_GROUND_Y, -335)` puts the wheels on the island ground. This is verified by the `dc8` scenario, which shows the closed plane parked at Gando.
   - The plane is about 65 m long (z −36.6…28) with a 42 m span. The fin top is at y 12.3 + 4.8 above the ground.
4. The scenery object also gets a read-only `phaseT` getter. It is additive.
5. `scene.onBeforeRender` is wrapped (chained) by flightScenery for the per-render camera checks. If anyone else sets it later, they should chain it too.
