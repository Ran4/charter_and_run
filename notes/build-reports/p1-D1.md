## Notes for the integrator / other block owners
- **Car vs ramp rule** (car.js `collideStatic`): every static piece overlapping the car's OBB is
  tested with its top **at the car centre** (ramps: projected and clamped onto their axis) against
  `car.y + 0.6`. So a ramp is drivable along its own axis at any slope, but pieces that rise
  *across* the travel direction act as walls once they are > 0.6 m higher anywhere under the body
  (~2.7 m ahead of the centre). Multi-piece terrain must keep slope ≤ 0.2 (my dunes do); D2's dune
  ramp at the lighthouse and any stepped decks should keep this in mind.
- Characters are fine on any slope; teleports and `approachSpot` snap to ground-level floors only,
  so I kept every landmark off raised floors (dansgolv is on the 0.4 deck, which counts as ground).
- The farmhouse azotea, the bygge slabs and the estanque rims are floors you can reach; the 3rd
  bygge slab (10.35) is unreachable by design.
- `MAT.mouth` (gfx.js, MeshBasic + vertex colours) is used for the glowing bulbs; if gfx changes it,
  the grisfest bulbs/embers need an equivalent unlit vertex-colour material.
- The spinners (pig, crane jib) use wall-clock time (`performance.now`), so they also turn while
  paused; purely decorative.
